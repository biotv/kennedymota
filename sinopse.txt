Kennedy Mota is an Automation Engineer with a final licenciatura’s grade (7.27 in 10) and having a Master degree in Industrial Automation Engineering, recognized by University of Aveiro (15 in 20). Currently, he has been enrolled in the doctoral program Electrical Engineering and Intelligent Systems at the University of Coimbra. Throughout his academic journey, he has consistently aimed to study at prestigious institutions. This ambition began with his Technical Electromechanical course at the Federal Center for Technological Education of Minas Gerais, CEFET-MG (2011-2013). Subsequently, he graduated in Automation Engineering from the Federal University of Rio Grande - FURG (2015-2019) and additionally he obtained a diploma in Technical of Industrial Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technology from the Federal Institute of Education, Science, and Technology of Rio Grande do Sul - Rio Grande Campus, IFRS (2018-2019). In 2022, he embarked on his doctoral journey at the University of Coimbra, aiming to further expand his expertise and make significant contributions to the fields of engineering and technology applied to mobile robotics.
In his professional journey, Kennedy has accumulated experience from his internships in the automotive industry to his current research at the University of Coimbra. He began as an Intern at FCA Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (06/2011-05/2013), focusing on process improvements and production lines using Lean Manufacturing and World Class Manufacturing (WCM) tools. Later, he worked as a Maintenance Assistant at INBRASP (11/2013-02/2014). During his internship at the Brazilian Navy (06/2019-11/2019), besides overseeing maintenance on vessels, including warships and other Brazilian Navy ships, he contributed to the development of a low-cost CNC machine to automate processes and repairs within the Military Organization (MO). He also participated in the development of an Alert System for the Rio Grande Naval Station as main developer.
Kennedy also gained experience as an IT Intern at the Federal University of Rio Grande - FURG (03/2016-02/2018), performing activities such as assembly, maintenance, and configuration of computers and servers. Additionally, he served as a Researcher at the same university (03/2021-12/2022), at the Coastal and Estuarine Oceanography Laboratory (LOCOSTE), assisting in the development of algorithms in Python and MATLAB for data processing. As an Autonomous Hardware Engineer (01/2020-08/2021), he focused on developing electronic circuit boards, IoT embedded systems, and applications servers in Docker containers.
In his journey, Kennedy worked as a Research Fellow at the Institute of Systems and Robotics at the University of Coimbra, from May 2023 to the present. In this role, he contributed to the GreenBotics project, focusing on intelligent robotic systems for digital agriculture. Previously, he worked as an Automation Analyst at Wagner, Zamberlam, and CIA LTDA. (09/2021-02/2023), developing hardware and software for equipment monitoring.
Kennedy's solid and wide-ranging experience has equipped him to deal with a variety of situations and challenges with motivation and problem-solving skills, both in research and development projects; therefore, he is very keen to pursue this new journey leveraging his hard-soft skills towards innovative contributions to the mobile robotic area.
